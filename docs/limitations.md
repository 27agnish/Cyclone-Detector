# CycloneShield AI - Limitations & Meteorological Disclaimers

## Operational Notice
**CycloneShield AI is a prototype decision-support research tool developed for disaster management and technology hackathons.**

1. **Not an Official Warning Service**: All cyclone tracks, forecast uncertainty cones, landfall estimates, and risk scores generated by this prototype are model-derived estimates. Emergency operations centers and citizens must strictly refer to official bulletins issued by the **India Meteorological Department (IMD)**, **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)**, and relevant State Disaster Management Authorities (e.g., OSDMA).
2. **Forecast Uncertainty**: Real cyclone trajectories are subject to environmental steering currents, vertical wind shear, and sea surface temperature anomalies. The displayed forecast uncertainty cone represents empirical error distributions and does not guarantee that the storm eye will remain within the cone boundaries.
3. **Data Freshness**: In DEMO mode, simulated scenarios (such as Cyclone DANA approaching coastal Odisha) are used to demonstrate multi-hazard decision support workflows without depending on active meteorological crises during evaluation.
