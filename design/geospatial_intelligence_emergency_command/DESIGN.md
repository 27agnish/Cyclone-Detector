## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, tactical command center interface designed for national-level disaster management teams, meteorologists, civil protection authorities, and emergency response coordinators. 

### Emotional Architecture
The interface projects unwavering operational authority, mission-critical clarity, and low cognitive overhead under high-stress conditions:
- **Absolute Precision:** Every visual indicator, coordinate read-out, and uncertainty threshold communicates mathematical fidelity and model certainty.
- **Urgent Restraint:** High saturation and warm spectrum accents are reserved exclusively for critical thresholds, vector threats, and real-time hazard alerts, preventing operator fatigue during prolonged operations.
- **Situational Immediacy:** Tactical telemetry, landfall probabilities, and geospatial layers are rendered with high information density, low latency visual states, and immediate hierarchy.

### Design Movement & Visual Language
The aesthetic fuses **Tactical Command Minimalism** with **Data-Dense Geospatial Hud Systems**:
- **Monochrome Charcoal Canvas:** Deep obsidian and slate backgrounds (#0B111E, #0F172A) minimize eye strain in 24/7 dark-room operations while maximizing map contrast.
- **Engineered Micro-Surfaces:** Thin structural boundaries (1px, high-precision slate alpha) replace blurred organic shadows, grounding analytical modular panels.
- **Instrument-Grade Telemetry:** Data density is supported through tabular mono-stacks, tight visual cadences, and distinct chromatic risk levels that align with global standard operating procedures for disaster command.

## Colors

The system uses a strictly orchestrated dark palette engineered for deep-contrast geospatial visualization and real-time telemetry rendering.

### Primary Operational Brand System
- **Primary Accent (`#0284C7`):** Primary interactive states, active track line trajectories, and structural selections.
- **Secondary Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Secondary sensor streams, telemetry readouts, radar vectors, and vector headings.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38BDF8`):** Hover rings, active uncertainty cone outlines, scrubber playhead markers, and crosshair reticles.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0B111E`):** Root background, underlying map viewports, bathymetric layers.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0F172A`):** Master sidebars, command docks, persistent telemetry panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1E293B`):** Modals, floating cards, inspector drawers, popovers.
- **Surface Level 3 / Hover (`#334155`):** Table row hover, active chip background, divider lines.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`):** Standard panel separation and structural data grids.
- **Border Focus (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`):** Active spatial tiles, focused inputs, target acquisitions.

### Typography & Content Neutrals
- **High-Contrast Text (`#F8FAFC`):** Headline metrics, critical alerts, primary data values.
- **Standard Text (`#94A3B8`):** Field labels, axis notations, secondary metadata, body copy.
- **Muted Text (`#64748B`):** Timestamp suffixes, inactive scrubber increments, table column headers.

### Standardized Threat & Risk Severity Spectrum
Semantic colors are non-negotiable and strictly reserved for hazard severity:
- **LOW (`#10B981`, bg: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`, border: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`): Baseline risk, stable sea conditions, fully operational infrastructure.
- **MODERATE (`#F59E0B`, bg: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, border: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`): Tropical depression advisory, secondary alert status, preparatory watch.
- **HIGH (`#F97316`, bg: `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`, border: `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`): Cyclone warning, mandatory shoreline staging, active gale force threshold.
- **CRITICAL (`#EF4444`, bg: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, border: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.45)`): Imminent landfall, life-threatening storm surge, Cat 4/5 sustained winds.

## Typography

The typography strategy separates structural linguistic context from mission-critical alphanumeric telemetry.

### Font Pairings
- **Primary Typeface (Inter):** Leveraged for all navigation, headings, advisory prose, and conversational alerts. Inter offers high structural legibility at small sizes with wide apertures that remain clear under high-speed scanning.
- **Telemetry & Technical Monospace (JetBrains Mono):** Standardized across latitude/longitude indices, sustained wind readings (knots/kmh), central barometric pressure (hPa), storm forward motion vectors, and probability percentages. Monospace widths guarantee that rapid data flickers do not induce optical layout jitter.

### Typographic Directives
- **Tabular Figures Required:** Ensure all numeric values employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned data columns in live sensor tables.
- **Capitalization Conventions:** Technical identifiers, state chips (`DEMO MODE`, `LIVE DATA`), coordinate fields, and column headers must use `text-transform: uppercase` paired with increased letter spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to enforce rapid scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a **Dense Cockpit Layout Model** optimized for large-format displays, operations center video walls, and high-resolution desktop terminals, with dedicated responsive collapsing rules for tactical tablets and mobile units.

### Canvas Grid Architecture
- **Master Operational Viewport (Desktop):** A 16-column continuous grid overlay. The GIS Nautical Canvas serves as the z-0 canvas, flanked by collapsable tactical docks:
  - Left Dock: 320px fixed width (Cyclone Selector, Sensor Streams, Wind Field Profiles).
  - Main Central GIS: Fluid width stretching 100% of remaining area.
  - Right Inspector Dock: 360px fixed width (Population Exposure, Infrastructure Risk Matrix, Evacuation Staging).
  - Floating Bottom Scrub Bar: Center-anchored fluid bar (Now through Landfall horizon).
- **Tablets (768px – 1024px):** Docks collapse into off-canvas sliding sheets triggered by floating tactical action buttons. The GIS canvas occupies 100% viewport width.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode. The map scales to a fixed 45vh viewport pinned to the top, while an accordion sheet hosts telemetry and critical threat cards beneath it.

### Spacing Rhythm & Density
- Spacing follows a rigorous base-4 grid system (2px, 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px).
- Internal component padding is kept compact (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to support high-density metric display without requiring vertical scroll under standard operational resolutions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, warm-tinted dropshadows; instead, it relies on **Structured Dark Surface Layering**, **High-Precision Outlines**, and **Tactical Alpha Paneling**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Surface Level 0 (Ground Canvas):** `#0B111E` — Nautical map viewport, radar bathymetry background, coordinate grid lines.
- **Surface Level 1 (Docked Structural Panels):** Background: `#0F172A` at 94% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`. Shadow: None.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Overlay Cards & Tooltips):** Background: `#1E293B` at 96% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.9)`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Command Modals & Emergency Focus Overlays):** Background: `#0F172A`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.5)`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`, accompanied by a primary-toned glow ring `0 0 0 1px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`.

### Border Integrity
Every container panel is bounded by crisp 1px borders. These borders establish strict structural containment, ensuring telemetry metrics never bleed across adjacent spatial units during real-time updates.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **Soft-Technical (Level 1)** geometry. 

- **Standard Base Radii (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to telemetry data chips, table rows, button containers, dropdown fields, and input blocks. This subtle corner truncation maintains an industrial, instrument-grade appearance.
- **Large Panels & Dialogs (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to master modal sheets, floating map overlays, and inspector drawers.
- **Terminal Radii (Pill / Full Round):** Strictly limited to real-time status pulses (e.g., live sensor radar sweeps, online beacon indicators) and timeline scrubber nodes.
- **Zero Radii (0px):** Applied to geospatial canvas splitters, crosshairs, coordinate reticles, and raw vector bounding boxes.

- **System Mode Indicators:** Height: 20px. Monospace 10px uppercase, letter-spacing `0.08em`.

- **Risk Severity Badges:** Pinned against the standardized 4-tier risk matrix (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL). Padding: 2px 6px. Corner radius: 3px.

### 3. Timeline Scrubber (Track Horizon Controller)
- **Track Rail:** Height: 4px, background `#1E293B`, finished with a `#0284C7` progress track.
- **Horizon Waypoints:** Key markers (`NOW`, `+6h`, `+12h`, `+24h`, `+48h`, `LANDFALL`). Text: JetBrains Mono 10px `#94A3B8`.
- **Scrubber Playhead:** 14px circular disc with an outer `#0284C7` ring, an inner `#F8FAFC` core, and a continuous vertical guideline that cuts across underlying temporal charts.

### 4. GIS Map Panel & Uncertainty Cone Overlays
- **Viewport Canvas:** Nautical dark bathymetry theme (`#080D1A` water bodies, `#151F30` landmasses, `#1E293B` coastlines).
- **Trajectory Vectors:** Active eye path rendered with a 2px `#06B6D4` solid stroke.
- **Uncertainty Cone:** Linear gradient fill starting from `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)` expanding to `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.05)`, bordered by a dashed `#38BDF8` 1.5px stroke.
- **Landfall Impact Target:** Concentric expanding rings keyed dynamically to the current threat tier (e.g., `#EF4444` for Critical).

### 5. Data Tables & Sensor Streams
- **Headers:** JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase, text `#64748B`, height 28px, border-bottom 1px `#1E293B`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#0F172A` to `#131D31`), row height: 32px, text `#F8FAFC` (12px Mono for numeric values, 13px Inter for named infrastructure).
- **Hover State:** Background `#1E293B` with a 2px left border accent in `#0284C7`.

### 6. Command Palette Modal (Emergency Quick-Action)

- **Dialog Box:** Max width: 640px. Background `#0F172A`, 1px border `#0284C7`.
- **Search Input:** Height 44px, JetBrains Mono 14px, icon indicator `#38BDF8`, no native outline, zero inner margins.
- **Action Results:** Segmented by categorical headers (`LAYERS`, `INFRASTRUCTURE`, `EVACUATION ZONES`). Selected item highlighted with `#1E293B` and an active `#06B6D4` left indicator.
